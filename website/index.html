Change your slides from your wrist

PPT Remote turns a Garmin Venu Sq 2 Music into a remote control for PowerPoint on your Windows PC. This page walks you through setting it up, one small step at a time. No experience needed.

What you need

Tick these off before you start. It only takes a minute.

  • A Garmin Venu Sq 2 Music (the one with “Music” in the name). The plain Venu Sq 2 is not supported.
  • A Windows PC with Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11. Almost any PC from the last few years is fine.
  • Bluetooth on your PC. Most laptops have it built in. If you have a desktop, you may need a small USB Bluetooth adapter.
  • The USB charging cable that came with your watch. We use it once to copy a file onto the watch.
  • PowerPoint, or any program where the arrow keys change slides (Google Slides in a web browser works too).

Good to know: you don’t need an internet connection while presenting, an account, or the Garmin Connect app on your phone. The watch and the PC talk to each other directly over Bluetooth.

1Download the two files

You need two files: one for your PC and one for your watch.

  1. Open the downloads page.
  2. Scroll down to the part called Assets. You may need to click the little arrow to open it.
  3. Click PptRemote-Windows-win-x64.zip to download the PC app.
    My PC isn’t sure which one to pick

    Most PCs need win-x64. Only a few newer laptops with “ARM” processors (some Surface and Snapdragon laptops) need win-arm64.

    To check: open Settings, then System, then About. Look at System type. If it says “x64-based processor”, pick win-x64. If it says “ARM-based processor”, pick win-arm64.

  4. Click PptRemote.prg to download the watch app.
  5. Both files usually land in your Downloads folder.

Your browser might say the file “isn’t commonly downloaded”. That’s normal for new, small programs. Choose Keep (in Edge: click the three dots, then Keep, then Keep anyway).

2Set up the app on your PC

Unzip it

  1. Open your Downloads folder.
  2. Right-click the zip file you downloaded, then choose Extract All…, then click Extract.
  3. A new folder opens. Move this folder somewhere you’ll remember, such as your Documents folder. Don’t leave it inside the zip file, because the app won’t work from there.

Turn on Bluetooth

  1. Open Settings and go to Bluetooth & devices.
  2. Make sure the Bluetooth switch at the top says On.
  3. You do not need to pair your watch here. Please don’t. The app handles the connection by itself.

Start the app

  1. In the folder, double-click PptRemoteServer.exe.
  2. You may see a blue window that says “Windows protected your PC”. Don’t worry. Click the words More info, then click the Run anyway button that appears.

    Why does Windows say that? Windows gets cautious about any program it hasn’t seen many times before, especially if the creator hasn’t paid for a digital signature. It doesn’t mean there is anything wrong with the app. You only need to do this the first time.

  3. A window called PPT Remote opens. The bar at the top should be blue and say “Waiting for your watch”. That means the PC is ready.
  4. Under WATCH PIN you’ll see a number, such as 482 913. Keep this window visible. You’ll type this number into your watch in Step 4.

If the bar at the top is red and says “Bluetooth problem”, jump to Something’s not working?.

If you close the window, the app keeps running. It tucks itself into the system tray, the row of tiny icons at the bottom-right of your screen near the clock. You might need to click the little ^ arrow to see it. Double-click the icon to bring the window back. To quit for real, right-click the icon and choose Quit.

3Put the app on your watch

You’ll copy the PptRemote.prg file onto the watch, a bit like copying a photo to a USB stick.

  1. Plug the small end of the watch’s USB cable into the watch, and the big end into your PC.
  2. Wait about 10 seconds. The watch screen may show a USB symbol. That’s fine.
  3. Open File Explorer (the yellow folder icon on your taskbar, or press Windows + E).
  4. On the left, click This PC. Your watch should appear as a device with a name like Venu Sq 2 Music. Double-click it.
  5. You’ll see one or two folders. Open the one called Internal Storage or Primary if you see it. Then open the folder called GARMIN, and inside it the folder called Apps.
  6. Open your Downloads folder in a second File Explorer window, then drag PptRemote.prg into the watch’s Apps folder. (Or right-click the file, choose Copy, then right-click inside the Apps folder and choose Paste.)
  7. Wait for the copy to finish (it takes a second). Then close both windows and unplug the watch.
  8. The watch may restart or show a short “updating” message. Give it a minute.

Find the app on your watch

Look for PPT Remote in your watch’s list of apps. It has a blue square icon with a white triangle. Press the button on the watch, or swipe up from the watch face, and scroll until you see it. Tap it to open.

Can’t find the watch in File Explorer? Try a different USB port, make sure the cable is pushed in firmly, and check that the watch isn’t in the middle of a workout or update. Some cables only charge and can’t carry files, so if nothing appears, try the cable that came in the box.

Can’t find a folder called Apps? Open the GARMIN folder and look again. It is always inside there. If there is genuinely none, ask for help (see the bottom of this page) and mention what folders you can see.

4Type the PIN on your watch

The first time you open PPT Remote on the watch, it shows a number pad and asks for the PIN. This stops other people’s devices from changing your slides.

  1. Look at the PC window and find the number under WATCH PIN.
  2. On the watch, tap the digits to match it. Little dots at the top show how many you’ve typed. Made a mistake? Tap DEL.
  3. Tap OK (it turns green once you’ve typed enough digits).
  4. After a moment the watch screen shows a green dot and says Connected. On the PC, the top bar turns green and says “Watch connected”. 🎉

Once your watch has connected, the PC hides the PIN behind dots so nobody can read it over your shoulder. Click Show next to it any time to see it again. The watch remembers the PIN, so you only do this once.

5Use it

  1. On the PC, open your presentation and start the slideshow (in PowerPoint, press F5, or use the watch’s START button).
  2. Click once on the slideshow so it’s the window you’re looking at. The remote sends key presses to whichever window is “in front”.
  3. Tap NEXT on the watch, and the slide changes. 🎉

What each button does

On the watchWhat happens
NEXT (big blue button, right) or the physical button on the sideNext slide
PREV (big button, left)Previous slide
STARTStart the slideshow from the beginning, and start a talk timer
BLACKTurn the screen black (tap again to bring the slides back). Handy when you want everyone to look at you
ENDEnd the slideshow, and clear the talk timer
The strip at the very topWhen connected: start or stop the talk timer. When not connected: try again. If it says “Check PIN”: type the PIN again

The button you press flashes white, and the watch gives a tiny buzz so you know it worked. One long buzz means it couldn’t send (usually not connected). Two buzzes mean the connection just dropped.

Leaving the app

Swipe right on the watch screen twice. The first swipe only shows “Swipe again to exit”, so you can’t close it by accident in the middle of a talk.

Every time after that

  1. Start PptRemoteServer.exe on the PC. (Or tick Start with Windows in its window and it will start by itself, tucked away in the tray.)
  2. Open PPT Remote on the watch.
  3. Wait for Connected, then present.

Tip for long talks: your watch screen switches itself off after a while, and that can close the app. Before a big presentation, go to the watch’s settings and make the screen stay on longer (look for Display, then Timeout).

Something’s not working?

Click a problem to see what to do. Start from the top. The first few fix most things.

The watch is stuck on “Searching…”
  1. Is the PPT Remote window open on your PC (or its icon in the tray)? If not, start PptRemoteServer.exe.
  2. Does the PC’s top bar say “Waiting for your watch”? If it’s red, see “Bluetooth problem” below.
  3. Is Bluetooth switched On in Windows (Settings, then Bluetooth & devices)?
  4. Move the watch closer to the PC (within a couple of metres).
  5. Tap the strip at the top of the watch screen where it says Retry.
  6. If it still won’t connect, quit the app on the watch (swipe right twice) and open it again. Then right-click the PC app’s tray icon, choose Quit, and start it again.
The watch says “Check PIN”

The PIN on the watch doesn’t match the one on the PC.

  1. On the PC, look at WATCH PIN. If it shows dots, click Show.
  2. On the watch, tap the strip at the top (it says Set PIN) and type the number again, then tap OK.

Still rejected after typing it carefully? On the PC, click New PIN and type the new number into the watch. That also resets a safety counter that can get out of step (for example if the watch’s clock was changed).

If someone typed the wrong PIN several times, the PC pauses for one minute. Wait a minute and try again.

The PC says “Bluetooth problem”
  • “No Bluetooth adapter found”: your PC has no Bluetooth. Plug in a USB Bluetooth adapter (look for one that says “Bluetooth 5” on the box).
  • “…does not support the BLE peripheral role”: your Bluetooth adapter is too old or its driver is limited. Updating Windows and the Bluetooth driver sometimes fixes it. A newer USB Bluetooth 5 adapter almost always does.
  • Anything else: turn Bluetooth off and on again in Windows settings, then quit and restart the PPT Remote app.
It says Connected, but the slides don’t change
  1. Click on the slideshow once so it is the window in front. The remote “types” keys into whichever window is active.
  2. In the PPT Remote window, check “Send keypresses to the active window” is ticked.
  3. Use the Test: send Next in 3 seconds button, then click on your slideshow within 3 seconds. If the slide changes, the PC side works and the problem is on the watch side. If not, try the next point.
  4. Is PowerPoint running as administrator? Windows blocks normal apps from typing into those. Close both, then right-click PptRemoteServer.exe and choose Run as administrator.
  5. Try the other key style: in the window, change Slide navigation keys to Page Up / Page Down.
It worked, then stopped after a while

Most often the watch screen went to sleep and closed the app. Open PPT Remote on the watch again and it will reconnect by itself. For long talks, make the watch screen stay on longer (see “Tip for long talks” above). Your PC’s Bluetooth might also have been switched off by a power-saving setting, so check it’s still on.

Windows said “Windows protected your PC”

That’s expected the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. See Step 2 for why this happens.

My antivirus deleted or blocked the file

Antivirus programs are sometimes jumpy about new programs that press keys for you (which is exactly what this one does). Download it again and tell your antivirus to allow it. If you’d rather not, you can build it yourself from the source code on the project’s GitHub page.

I copied the file but the app isn’t on my watch
  1. Check the file went into the Apps folder inside GARMIN on the watch, not somewhere else.
  2. The file must be called exactly PptRemote.prg. If it was downloaded twice, it might be called PptRemote (1).prg. Rename it.
  3. Unplug the watch, wait a minute, and look again. Restarting the watch (hold the side button, then choose to power off and on) helps if it still doesn’t appear.
  4. Make sure the watch really is a Venu Sq 2 Music. The app is built for that model only.
I forgot my PIN

It’s in the PPT Remote window on your PC under WATCH PIN. If it shows dots, click Show.

None of this helped

Please open an issue on GitHub and tell us: what the PC’s top bar says, what the top strip of the watch says, your Windows version, and what you’ve already tried. Copy a few lines from the ACTIVITY list at the bottom of the PC window if you can.

Is this safe?

  • Nothing goes over the internet. The watch and the PC talk directly over Bluetooth, a short distance apart.
  • The PIN protects your slides. The PC ignores any message that isn’t signed with your PIN, so a stranger’s phone or laptop can’t change your slides. The PC also locks out guessing for a minute after five wrong tries.
  • No accounts, no tracking. The app doesn’t collect anything.
  • What it can do: press a handful of keys (arrow keys, F5, Esc, B) on your PC. That’s all. And only while the app is running.

One honest limitation: the Bluetooth link itself isn’t encrypted. A short PIN is plenty to stop casual, passing devices. It wouldn’t stop a determined person standing nearby who is recording your Bluetooth traffic. If that worries you, click New PIN in the PC app and choose a 12-digit PIN.

You can read every line of the code on the project’s GitHub page.

How to remove it

  1. On the PC: right-click the tray icon and choose Quit. If you ticked Start with Windows, untick it first. Then delete the folder you extracted. That’s everything on the PC side.
  2. On the watch: plug it in, open GARMIN then Apps, and delete PptRemote.prg. Unplug it.
